Role System Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Role System Bot is a Discord bot that allows authorized server users to assign roles to members through an interactive embed and selection menus in a dedicated role assignment channel.

2. Information We Process

The bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, and interaction data required to identify the selected member and assign the selected role.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate the role assignment system, process user interactions, verify selections, and perform role assignments according to the bot's permissions and the server's role hierarchy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of the platform's normal operation. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The bot processes information as needed to perform its role assignment functionality. If the bot stores information in files, databases, or other services, that storage and retention should be disclosed accurately. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the bot owner through Discord.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Role System Bot owner through Discord.
