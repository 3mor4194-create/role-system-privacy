# Privacy Policy — Role System Bot **Last updated:** October 10, 2026 ## 1. About the Bot Role System Bot helps authorized users assign roles to server members through interactive embeds and selection menus. ## 2. Information Processed The bot processes Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, and interaction data as necessary to identify the selected member and assign the selected role. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used only to provide the bot's role assignment functionality, process interactions, and maintain the security of the service. ## 4. Data Storage and Sharing The bot does not intentionally store personal information in a separate database or persistent storage system. Information is processed as needed to perform the requested role assignment. Information is not sold to third parties. ## 5. Data Retention and Deletion Interaction data is processed as needed to operate the bot. If you have questions about data handling or deletion, contact the bot owner through Discord. ## 6. Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. ## 8. Contact For privacy questions or data handling requests, contact the bot owner through Discord.
