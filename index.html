# Privacy Policy — Role System Bot **Last updated:** October 10, 2026 ## 1. About the Bot Role System Bot is a Discord bot that helps authorized users assign roles to server members through an interactive embed and selection menus. ## 2. Information Processed The bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, and interaction data required to identify the selected member and assign the selected role. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used only to provide the bot's role assignment functionality, process user interactions, and maintain the security of the service. ## 4. Data Storage and Sharing The bot owner should disclose whether any information is stored outside Discord, including in files, databases, or hosting services. Information is not sold to third parties. ## 5. Data Retention and Deletion Users who have questions or wish to request deletion of stored data can contact the bot owner through Discord. Requests will be reviewed and handled where applicable. ## 6. Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. ## 8. Contact For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot owner through Discord.
